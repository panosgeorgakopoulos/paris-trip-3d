# Paris 3D Trip Guide — December 1–3, 2026

Interactive single-file 3D Paris itinerary built for **Panos, Kostas, Iakovos & Imbrixis**, starting from **Rue Saint-Hyacinthe 3, 75001 Paris**.

🌐 **Live Site**: https://panosgeorgakopoulos.github.io/paris-trip-3d/

## Highlights
- **Real 3D Paris Cityscape**: 26,928 real OpenStreetMap building footprints, 11,796 real Paris streets across all 20 arrondissements, sculpted hills (Montmartre, Panthéon, Chaillot, Belleville), and a clear running-water River Seine shader.
- **Hand-Crafted Sights, Churches & Squares**: Eiffel Tower (with 18:00/19:00 sparkle), Louvre, Palais Garnier, Musée d'Orsay, Rodin, Invalides, Notre-Dame, Sainte-Chapelle, Panthéon, Sacré-Cœur, Grand Palais, Centre Pompidou, Tour Montparnasse, all formal gardens, and 20+ Parisian *Places*.
- **Animated 4-Friend Squad**: Play/pause the 4 friends walking along real Parisian routes across all 3 days with Follow-Cam, speed controls, and transit modes.
- **Mobile-First & Google Maps 3D Gestures**: 1-finger pan with inertia, 2-finger pinch/twist/tilt, double-tap zoom, compact peek cards, and offline `localStorage` checklists.
